KHUC THUY DU - DU TU LE

ABSTRACT

This project aims to create an immersive visual representation of the opening stanza of Du Tử Lê's poem Khúc Thụy Du. Through powerful metaphors and haunting imagery, the poem explores the futility of searching for meaning in the midst of war, pain, and human suffering. By combining interactive storytelling, layered parallax animation, atmospheric sound, and symbolic visuals, the project brings the poem's opening verses to life, inviting users to experience its emotional intensity and reflect on the fragility of life and hope.
