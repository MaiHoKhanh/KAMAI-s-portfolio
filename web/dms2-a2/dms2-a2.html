HOW TO MAKE CHINESE DUMPLINGS (Jiaozi)?

ABSTRACT

This abstract music video explores the transformation of graphic motion into complex repeated patterns. I aimed to visualize low melody as a geometric transformation through simple forms, mainly circles, and build up frequency as repeated forms to create a transition from basic motion to hypnotized patterns. The color palette remains a mixture of cool tones and warm colors in different contrasts throughout the whole soundtrack, representing the emotional arc of the track. By focusing on this steady development, the video invites viewers to observe how the basic motion is constructed and turned into symmetrical patterns. The scope is limited to abstract motion built from simple geometric shapes that gradually synchronize to repeated patterns.
